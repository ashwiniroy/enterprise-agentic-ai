ENTERPRISE CUSTOMER SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE

1. ORDER SUPPORT
Customers can contact support for questions about order status, delivery delays, missing items, damaged products, cancellations, and invoice requests.

Order Status:
- Processing: The order has been received and is being prepared.
- Shipped: The order has left the fulfillment center.
- Delivered: The carrier has marked the order as delivered.
- Cancelled: The order was cancelled and will not be shipped.

If an order is delayed by more than 5 business days beyond the estimated delivery date, support should verify the carrier status before offering a resolution.

2. RETURNS
Most eligible products can be returned within 30 calendar days from the delivery date.
Products should be returned with their original accessories and packaging when reasonably possible.
Products damaged through misuse may not qualify for a standard return.

3. REFUNDS
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method.
After approval, refunds are generally initiated within 2 business days.
Banks and card providers may take an additional 3 to 7 business days to display the refund.

4. WARRANTY SUPPORT
Warranty coverage varies by product.
Customers should provide the order number, product name, purchase date, and a description of the defect.
Physical damage caused by accidents, misuse, or unauthorized modification is not covered by the standard manufacturer warranty.

5. DAMAGED OR DEFECTIVE PRODUCTS
If a customer receives a damaged product, support should request:
- Order number
- Product name
- Description of the damage
- Photographs when available

For products reported defective after delivery, support should first check the product warranty and troubleshooting guidance.

6. ESCALATIONS
Escalate to a support specialist when:
- A refund exceeds $500.
- The customer disputes a previous refund decision.
- A safety concern is reported.
- The issue involves repeated failures after two support attempts.

7. CUSTOMER PRIVACY
Support agents should not request passwords, full payment-card numbers, or authentication secrets.
Only collect the minimum information required to resolve the case.
